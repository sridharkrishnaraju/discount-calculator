# Guide: How discount maths works

Final price = original × (1 − discount ÷ 100). To go the other way — you know the sale price and want the percent off — divide the saving by the original and multiply by 100. Stacked discounts multiply, they don't add: 20% then 10% off is 0.8 × 0.9 = 28% off, not 30%.
